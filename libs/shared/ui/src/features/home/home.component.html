Why teams use it

  • One intake channel for requests instead of scattered chat messages.
  • Clear ownership, priority, status, and history for every ticket.
  • Faster handoff between support agents, admins, and requesters.
  • Reporting that shows volume, bottlenecks, and recurring issues.

What to watch

  • Tickets need good titles and descriptions to avoid slow triage.
  • Status updates must be maintained or users lose trust in the queue.
  • Complex incidents may still require live communication outside the ticket.
  • Access roles should be reviewed so sensitive requests stay protected.

Service flow

From request to accountable work

  1. 1 Submit request

    User describes the issue, impact, and urgency.

  2. 2 Triage

    Support checks priority, category, duplicates, and missing details.

  3. 3 Assign owner

    The ticket moves to the right agent or team for resolution.

  4. 4 Resolve

    Work is completed, documented, and shared with the requester.
